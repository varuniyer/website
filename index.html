Varun Iyer

PhD student in Computer Science at the University of Illinois Chicago

At the University of Illinois Chicago I work with Professor Cornelia Caragea and study inference-time methods for zero-shot abstractive document summarization. I earned my MSE at Johns Hopkins University, where I worked on NLP with Professor Benjamin Van Durme at the Center for Language and Speech Processing. As an undergraduate at the University of Massachusetts Amherst, I researched NLP with Professor Andrew McCallum. I also completed research internships at Amazon Alexa AI and the USC Information Sciences Institute.
